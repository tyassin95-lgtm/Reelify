Reelify bundled music and sound effects
=======================================

All music tracks in assets/audio/music and all sound effects in assets/audio/sfx are ORIGINAL
works generated procedurally by tools/generate_audio.py in the Reelify source repository
(synthesized pads, plucked tones, bass, drums and filtered noise — no samples, loops or
recordings from third parties were used).

They are dedicated to the public domain under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). You may use them in commercial and
non-commercial reels without attribution.

Imported music: tracks you import from your device are your responsibility. Only use music
you have the rights to publish.
