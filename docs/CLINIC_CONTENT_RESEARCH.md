# Aesthetic Dermatology & Laser Clinic Content Research (Instagram Reels & Snapchat)

## Purpose

This research informs Reelify 2.1's template refinements, hook engineering, clinical brief generation, and 420+ topic library so scripts reflect what actually earns saves, shares, DMs, and patient trust for real dermatology and laser practitioners—instead of generic spa slogans.

---

## 1. What Makes Medical Aesthetic Short-Form Video Perform Without Losing Credibility

1. **Single concrete mechanism or patient question per video**
   - Winning clinic Reels focus tightly on *one* specific insight: e.g., why picosecond pulses rely on photoacoustic pressure (`<400 ps`) rather than slow thermal burning, why red post-acne marks react differently than pitted icepick scars, or why CFU Èlife ultrasound works year-round because sound waves ignore surface melanin.
2. **Pattern-interrupt hooks grounded in clinical truth (First 1.5–3.0 seconds)**
   - Underperforming videos open with slow warm-ups (*"Hi guys, today I want to talk about…"*) or hype (*"Unlock the secret to radiant flawless skin!"*).
   - Effective clinician hooks start *inside* the patient's misconception or fear:
     - **Myth contradiction**: *"Calling CFU Èlife a laser misleads patients—here is why sound waves behave completely differently."*
     - **Direct blunt Q&A**: *"Will pico laser erase a dense black tattoo in two sessions? Honest answer: no."*
     - **Visual prep / safety reveal**: *"Look why we never rush past numbing cream and stainless test spots before Dermablate passes."*
3. **Show-then-explain over B-roll (`treatment_showcase` & mid-shot B-roll VO)**
   - Audiences distrust floating talking heads reciting marketing copy; showing real clinic workflows (marking facial vectors safely ahead of HIFU, IntelliTrak roller passes followed by sterile ampoules, non-squeezing suction filter results) pairs visual proof with calm scientific explanation.
4. **Honesty about downtime, suitability limits, and failure modes**
   - Candidly naming *"expect 3–7 days of visible healing"*, *"normal sandpaper grit on day 3"*, *"not suitable during active acne flare-ups"*, or *"deep pitted scars need resurfacing rather than facial polishing"* drives higher-trust bookings and comment saves than exaggerated "zero-downtime miracle" claims.
5. **Restraint on repetitive legal/consultation boilerplate**
   - Putting *"results depend on individual assessment"* in all three sections crowds out education and feels robotic. Reelify 2.1 limits qualification statements to once per reel, placing real mechanism, sensation, or pre/post-care value inside Shot 1 and Shot 2.

## 2. Platform Nuances: Instagram Reels vs Snapchat (Spotlights & Stories)

| Dimension | Instagram Reels | Snapchat Stories / Spotlight |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook cadence** | Sharp, high-contrast headline + visual contradiction in ≤ 3s (`Expert Explains`, `Myth Buster`, `3 Things`). | Conversational, close-lens clinician check-in as if messaging one patient directly (`Patient Question`, `Behind-the-Scenes`). |
| **Educational payoff** | Structured save-worthy checklists (`3 things to stop before laser`, numbered takeaways `1, 2, 3`, honest comparison tables). | Candid expectation setting, immediate post-session reality check ("5 minutes after treatment"), and approachable Iraqi dialect flow. |
| **Safe-zone framing & captions** | Top/bottom UI safe margins protected; clean burned captions (`Clean Clinical`, `Bold Engagement`, `Premium Minimal`) aid muted office scrolling. | Natural direct eye contact near upper third; shorter punchy spoken segments paced to avoid empty silence at end of section. |
| **Iraqi dialect fit** | Baghdad/urban Iraqi phrasing (`شنو يفرق`, `مو ليزر`, `بدون ما يقشّر`) blended accurately with clear technical terms (`PicoStar`, `1064 nm`, `HIFU`). | Intimate conversational warmth (`لا تستعجل بالحكم`, `الأمانة الطبية نحجيها بصراحة`) without stiff Modern Standard Arabic translationese. |

## 3. Mapping Research Into Reelify 2.1 Determinism

All visual template structures remain deterministic (3 equal sections = 10s, 15s, or 20s each) so recording guides, teleprompter windows, camera framing, captions, and rendering behave predictably:

1. **`expert_explains`**: Pattern-interrupt hook challenging an assumption -> B-roll VO teaching exact physics/biology (`FactualBriefBuilder`) -> Single practical qualification + calm takeaway CTA.
2. **`myth_buster`**: Voice the patient myth bluntly & mark FALSE immediately -> Correct with verified device/tissue mechanism -> Memorable consumer rule before booking.
3. **`treatment_showcase`**: Context hook over procedure setup -> Step-by-step live B-roll walkthrough (prep, tip/fluence calibration, comfort) -> Real immediate recovery appearance & candid aftercare.
4. **`three_things`**: Numbered concrete 1 / 2 / 3 framework with ZERO vague adjectives; every point anchors to a verified precaution, comparison, or biology variable.
5. **`patient_question`**: Verbatim clinic FAQ in Shot 1 -> Direct answer + *physiological why* over B-roll -> Individual determinant that alters planning + honest contraindication.
6. **`problem_approach`**: Relatable visible presentation -> Clinical evaluation & right-tool matching (plus acknowledging where another device fits better) -> Multi-week expectation + home SPF/barrier safeguard.
